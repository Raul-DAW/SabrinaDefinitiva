Biography

Sabrina Carpenter grew up in a small town in Pennsylvania with her parents and her three older sisters, Sarah, Shannon, and Cayla. She had a happy childhood surrounded by her family and nature. As a child, she was very creative, funny, and full of energy. She enjoyed dancing and spending time with her sisters, who were very important to her. From an early age, she showed a special interest in singing and performing in front of her family. Her parents encouraged her to follow her dreams and supported her artistic interests. At the age of ten, she began posting videos of herself singing on YouTube. This allowed her to share her talent with other people and take her first steps towards becoming a professional artist.

As a young girl, Sabrina was inspired by famous artists like Christina Aguilera and Adele. She loved listening to different types of music and discovering new ways to express herself. Unlike most teenagers, she spent much of her time preparing for auditions and working on her artistic skills. Her passion for performing eventually led her to Los Angeles, where she began exploring opportunities in the entertainment industry. In 2011, she made her first television appearance in Law & Order: Special Victims Unit. This experience was an important step towards her future career and helped her gain confidence in front of the cameras.

As Sabrina grew older, her music career became an important part of her life. In 2015, she released her first studio album, *Eyes Wide Open*, which helped her introduce her music to a wider audience. Over the following years, she continued developing her own style and writing songs about love, emotions, and personal experiences. Her album *Emails I Can't Send*, released in 2022, marked a new chapter in her career and showed a more mature side of her music. In 2025, her songs *Espresso* and *Please Please Please* became international hits, making her one of the most popular pop artists of her generation. Despite her success, Sabrina continues to express her personality through her music, fashion, and sense of humor.
